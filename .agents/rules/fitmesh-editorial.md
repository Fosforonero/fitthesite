# FitMesh editorial rules (Gemini Antigravity)

This block is generated from the FitMesh Governance repository. Do not edit it
by hand: edit the source and re-export. Everything outside the block markers in
the consuming file is preserved untouched.

## Always

- Do not use em dashes in FitMesh-authored user-facing copy.
- Do not mention internal AI tools, models or agents in user-facing copy,
  unless AI itself is the documented product feature or subject.
- Do not publish unverified future dates, availability claims or promises.

## Before you write

Before creating, translating or editing user-facing copy, read the applicable
documents under `.fitmesh/governance/standards/` completely.

The core applies everywhere. `standards/APP-WRITING.md` and
`standards/SITE-WRITING.md` are addenda for the application and the site, and
`standards/TRANSLATIONS.md` applies whenever a language boundary is crossed.
An addendum may make a rule stricter. It never makes one looser.

## Before you ship

Run the review in `.fitmesh/governance/workflows/EDITORIAL-REVIEW.md`. If the
copy asserts anything about what the product does, supports, protects or is
required to do, run `.fitmesh/governance/workflows/CLAIM-REVIEW.md` as well.
